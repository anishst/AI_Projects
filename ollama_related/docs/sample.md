# Pizza Palace Employee Handbook

Welcome to Pizza Palace! This handbook covers the basics of working at our restaurant, from
dough-making to closing procedures. It is intentionally full of headings, short and long
paragraphs, lists, a table, and a code block so it can be used to exercise different
text-chunking strategies (fixed-size, recursive-character, semantic, markdown-header-aware, etc.).

## 1. Company Overview

Pizza Palace was founded in 2010 with a simple goal: serve the best thin-crust pizza in town
using fresh, locally sourced ingredients. We now have three locations and a small central
kitchen that handles dough production for all of them.

Our mission is to combine old-world technique with modern efficiency. Every location bakes
pizza in a wood-fired oven imported from Naples, but we track inventory, scheduling, and
orders through a modern point-of-sale system.
